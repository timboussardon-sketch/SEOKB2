# Prospect pre-audit, before a call

## Quand utiliser ce skill

Prepares a full SEO/GEO pre-audit of a prospect or a client BEFORE a call,
from their URL alone and from entirely public data (no paid tool, no Search
Console needed). Runs a collection protocol, applies a grid of 7 business
insights, and produces a structured document ready for the call (diagnosis,
angle, SEO state, multi-axis pSEO strategy, 90-day roadmap, call notes).
Absolute rule: no invented volume, position or traffic figure.

ALWAYS use this skill when the user says: "prepare the pre-call", "do a
pre-audit", "audit before the call", "analyse this prospect", "get me ready
for this client", "pre-call on [site]", or hands over a prospect or client URL
and asks for a strategic read before a sales conversation.

This produces the document you prepare BEFORE a call with a prospect or a new client. It turns a
bare URL into something you can act on, with no private access: no Google Search Console, no paid
tool. Everything rests on what is public, plus structured business reasoning.

## The base principle

A good pre-audit does not recite data. It settles two or three sharp strategic calls: which ground
this site can defend, which business problem is costing them money, and where to start. The rest
(volumes, positions, traffic) gets confirmed during the call, by asking for access to the data. It
is never simulated.

## The hard rule, not negotiable

No search volume, no position, no traffic figure is invented. Any unverified number is marked
`[TO SOURCE]` or "to get on the call". The prospect's Search Console is almost never available
beforehand: list it as a question to ask, do not guess at it. One invented figure in a pre-audit
costs you your credibility at the first check.

## What fires this

The user hands over a URL, or a name and a URL, and asks to prepare the pre-call. Run the three
phases in order: collection (A), the 7-insight grid (B), the output skeleton (C).

---

## Phase A. The collection protocol, before any reasoning

Gather what is public, in this order, noting the source of every fact.

1. **Identity and business.** Main site and any linked subdomains, the services or offers page,
   legal pages, LinkedIn, public company registers (incorporation date, size, headcount,
   accreditations). What you want: how long they have existed, how big they are, how many business
   lines they cover, who decides.

2. **How they make money.** Who pays, for what, likely order value, sales cycle (inbound lead,
   tender, word of mouth), and the real conversion point (quote, demo, booking, purchase).

3. **Technical SEO state, without a paid tool.** Open `/sitemap.xml` and the CMS sitemaps, count
   the useful pages per section, spot the geographic or decisional pages that already exist, check
   freshness (lastmod), read the URL architecture. A rebuild in progress is a major signal.

4. **GEO surface.** Does this business generate decisional queries that a generative answer does
   not eat (niche B2B, proprietary data), or informational queries that AI swallows whole? That is
   what sets the angle.

5. **Proprietary data.** Own products, a method, client cases, field work, internal figures. That
   is the fuel against AI, and what makes the site defensible.

6. **To ask on the call, never simulated.** Search Console access (monthly impressions, average
   position, top 10, the queries that pay, leads attributed to SEO), what share of revenue each
   business line carries, who decides, who runs the rebuild.

Other modules worth calling during collection and reasoning, if they are installed: `geo-audit`.
The pSEO, decisional-keyword, objection and vector-entity modules are not part of this kit. Call
what you have to structure the analysis, never to dress it up.

---

## Phase B. The grid of 7 business insights, the heart of it

This is where the call is won. Answer each line factually from the collection. If an answer is
weak, mark it as a question for the call. A pre-audit has done its job when two or three of these
are settled sharply.

| # | Insight | The question you ask | What comes out |
|---|---------|----------------------|----------------|
| 1 | **Defensible niche** | Which business line or offer is hard to attack (few national players, fully decisional intent, proprietary data, queries AI does not eat)? | The ground you attack first, and why not the most contested generalist line. |
| 2 | **The pain that costs money** | Which unspoken business problem are they losing on today (leads going elsewhere, dependence on word of mouth, a site rebuilt pretty and invisible, spread across too many trades)? | The internal diagnosis. Reframed as "what this costs you", never repeated to the prospect as is. |
| 3 | **The multi-axis pSEO lever** | How do you cut each business line into axes (by city, by type of organisation, by feature, by problem), and where is the crossing that creates authority? | The concrete strategy: 4 to 6 page models, ranked (see skeleton C.4). |
| 4 | **The path to the lead** | Each page created points where? Where is the conversion point? Would a free tool (an audit, a calculator) capture the decision-maker's email at the right moment? | The argument that this brings inbound requests, not that it brings traffic. |
| 5 | **The timing, the opening hook** | What makes now the moment (a rebuild under way, a product launch, seasonality, lost positions)? | The first sentence of the call. A rebuild under way means integrating the SEO architecture costs almost nothing right now. |
| 6 | **The likely objections** | What is going to block (already have an agency, we build our own site, SEO is slow, budget)? | The prepared answer to each one, ready without hesitating. |
| 7 | **The exit from the call** | What does the prospect leave with? Which engagement format fits their maturity? | The close: a dated 90-day roadmap and the matching proposal. |

No bullshit: you do not talk about "visibility", you talk about AI citations, positions, leads and
conversions. No invented figure. Insight 2, the pain, stays an internal read; you do not put it to
the prospect head on.

## Phase C. The output skeleton, to fill in

The order is fixed: summary, then diagnosis, angle, SEO state, multi-axis pSEO, 90-day roadmap,
call notes, to verify. Always open with the summary block. No em dashes anywhere. Direct, factual,
in the author's own voice.

```markdown
# Pre-audit — <Prospect> (<domain>)

## In short

[3 to 5 sentences. Who they are, the SEO state in one line, the signal or the
proof of a pattern if there is one, the opportunity in one sentence (the
multi-axis pSEO lever), where to start and why. End on the call hook and the
recommended exit.]

Sources: [URLs and readings, with the date]. SEO: [sitemaps read on YYYY-MM-DD].
Search Console not available (prospect not connected), to get on the call.

## 1. Prospect diagnosis

**Identity.** [Location, how long, size, accreditations, B2B or B2C target.]
**Business lines and offers.** [The trades, and the sites attached to them.]
**Model and conversion.** [Who pays, for what, the real conversion point.]
**Likely pain (internal read, not to repeat as is).** [Insight 2.]
**To get on the call (Search Console).** [Monthly impressions, average position,
top 10, the queries that pay, SEO leads. No figure put forward until Search
Console has been read.]

## 2. The angle: the defensible niche first

[Insight 1: the line to attack first, and why. Defensibility (few players,
decisional intent, queries AI does not eat), the proprietary data that actually
exists, the potential for a free tool. Then the second lever, then what you do
NOT do first (the contested generalist line). End on the timing, insight 5.]

## 3. SEO state of play (sitemaps, YYYY-MM-DD)

[Per site or section: number of useful pages, what exists, what is empty. The
key signal: a geographic or decisional page already there, proving a pattern
that was never rolled out. That is the pSEO opening.]

## 4. The pSEO strategy: crossing the axes

[The principle: authority comes from crossing several cutting axes per business
line, not from a single model. Then, per line, list the models M1, M2, M3. For
each model:]

**M<n> — <Axis A> × <Axis B>** (intent targeted)
- URL: /<pattern>/. Examples of real pages.
- Variable: <the variable>. Estimated volume: ~N pages.
- Data per page: [what fills the page, from the field].
- Dedup and anti-thin: [what already exists, the rule against an empty page].

### Ranking

| Model | Possible pages | Effort | Competition | Intent / conversion | Data available | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M… | ~N | Low/Medium/High | Low/Medium/High | Strong/Very strong | Strong/Medium/Weak | 1-5 |

Recommendation: [where to start, in which order, why].

### Example queries (volumes to source, never invented)
- M1: [...]
- M2: [...]

### The 7 pSEO rules applied
Anti-thin, field data with no invented figure, sourcing required, clean
canonical, differentiated linking per page, at least one high-surprise element
per section, a 150 to 200 word anchored passage with authorship on each page.

## 5. The 90-day roadmap

- Weeks 1-2: [scoping, variables, the query list per axis].
- Weeks 3-4: [vector entities and clustering on the pillars, building the
  templates].
- Weeks 5-8: [ship the pilot of the priority model, cross-linking, resubmit to
  Search Console, check indexing].
- Weeks 9-12: [measure in Search Console, keep what indexes and performs, extend
  the winning model, wire in the free tool].

## 6. Notes for the call (<length>)

- Opening hook: "[insight 5, in words]"
- Questions to ask: [who decides, web leads against other sources, Search
  Console access, who runs the rebuild, what share of revenue each line carries].
- Likely objections to clear: "[objection]" → [the answer]. (insight 6)
- Close: leave with the dated roadmap and the proposal that matches their
  maturity. (insight 7)

## 7. To verify, and what comes next

- [Sitemaps or pages to open and confirm.]
- Get Search Console on the call, to set the volumes, the target hierarchy and
  the day-90 figures.
- Produce the real list of decisional queries per priority axis, and the page
  template of the winning model.
```

## Quality checklist before handing it over

- The summary block opens the document and settles the opportunity in one sentence.
- Two or three insights from the grid are settled sharply, not buried.
- No invented figure: every volume, position and traffic number is sourced or marked to get.
- The angle says plainly where you start AND what you do not do first.
- Every pSEO model has a real variable and data that fills the page. No empty page.
- The opening hook and the answers to objections are ready to say out loud.
- No empty jargon, no "visibility", no em dashes.

## Pre-decision output (required)

This module's table is not the end. Its output also carries a decision: a one-sentence diagnosis (the structural cause), 3 to 5 figures, a state (critical / to improve / no major issue detected, with the real data coverage), problems (3 max) and opportunities kept apart, actions P1 to P3 (3 P1 max) with why, evidence, expected signal, effort low / medium / high, confidence, risk, "Validation required" for any deletion, 301 or merge. What the data cannot support. Full format: `../audit-report/FORMAT.md`; the `audit-report` module assembles every module's decision into the final report.

Rules: every statement is a FACT, a HYPOTHESIS or an ACTION; a problem exists once; an anomaly is not a priority by default; business pages and Search Console trends before secondary technical anomalies; never a merge, 301, deletion, rewrite or new page recommended automatically; never a score out of 10, an estimate in hours or a promised figure; short content alone is never a problem; the certainty shown never exceeds the data coverage; no action on a page whose queries are unknown.
