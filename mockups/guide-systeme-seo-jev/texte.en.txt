@@skill
name: seo-mots-cles-business

description: Sorts a site's keywords by business intent, with or without Search Console. Three gates, five decisions, no invented volume. Triggers: "business keywords", "which keywords to target", "what should my site fight for".

# Business keywords

## What you give Claude

- The offer and the conversion point (quote, appointment, purchase).
- The target, the market, the language.
- The URLs of the offer pages.
- The Search Console export, Queries + Pages, 3 to 6 months, if you have it.
- Client verbatims, if you have any.

## Instruction

> Run seo-mots-cles-business on [URL]. Offer: [...], target: [...], conversion: [...], market: [...].

## Steps

1. List the non-brand queries. Group the variants of one need into one intent. Flag each merge.

2. Without Search Console, collect Google Suggest, web search and verbatims. Two queries that share the same top 10 go on one page.

3. Discard what an AI answers without a click: definition, "how to", single fact. Keep purchase, price, comparison, reviews, local.

4. Run each intent through the three gates:
- Matches the offer? Yes / No.
- Business intent? Strong / Medium / Weak, read from the modifiers, never from the volume.
- Demonstrated opportunity? Already captured / New / Not demonstrated.

5. Decide: PUSH, CREATE, CONSOLIDATE, DISCARD or TO VALIDATE.

## Observed demand

Write Weak / Medium / Strong, with the signals named next to it.

Strong: 5 Suggest variants or more, 7 mentions in calls or more.

Only 20 Search Console impressions or more confirm the demand.

## Output

- Diagnosis in one sentence.
- 3 to 5 figures, with source and period.
- Four tables: captured intents, opportunities with no page, underperforming pages, discarded queries with their reason.
- 3 actions maximum, each justified by its gate.
- What the data does not allow you to conclude.

## Rules

- No invented volume, position or CPC.
- No score out of 10.
- A high-volume query outside the offer ends up DISCARDED, reason written.
- Search Console does not show the whole market.
- No page created by reflex. First look for an existing URL or existing content.

Save the output in `YYYY-MM-DD-<site>-mots-cles-business.md`.

description: Audits and builds a site's internal linking without Search Console. Triggers: "audit the internal linking", "orphan pages", "linking plan".

# Internal linking

## What you give Claude

- The sitemap URL, or the list of articles.
- Your 3 to 5 pillar keywords.
- Your offer pages: audit, contact, quote, tool.

## Instruction

> Audit the internal linking of mysite.com. My offer pages: [URL].

## Step 0: crawl

python3 ~/.claude/skills/maillage-systeme/scripts/crawl_graph.py https://mysite.com/sitemap.xml -o ./maillage-data

Only `index.json` enters the conversation.

A link present on 80% of pages or more is a template link. It doesn't count.

## Steps

1. Classify each page: Know or Do.

2. Group into pillars of 3 pages minimum, with one hub each.

3. Count body links by distinct source pages. Spot orphans and dead ends.

4. For each link to create: passage and 3 anchors (exact, partial, semantic).

5. Rank them:
- orphan offer page;
- Know to Do link;
- hub to satellite;
- orphan outside the offer;
- link between pillars.

## Criteria for an anchor

- It says what the target promises.
- The sentence still reads well without the link.
- It is not already used toward the same target.
- The 5 words around it are about the target's topic.

One failed criterion: the anchor is rejected.

## Output

- Pillars and hubs.
- Table: page, inbound, outbound, status.
- Link plan: source, target, passage, anchors, justification.
- Diagnosis in one sentence, 3 P1 actions maximum.
- What the data does not allow you to conclude.

## Before adding the links

- Read each passage out loud without the link.
- Insert in batches of 10 to 20.
- Rerun the crawl after each batch.
- End criterion: zero orphan offer pages.

## Rules

- No numeric score.
- An orphan legal page is not a problem.
- Links in the text, never in a "See also".
- Never a link to the homepage from the content.

Save the output in `YYYY-MM-DD-<site>-maillage.md`.
@@end

description: Sets up or audits a site's JSON-LD structured data, derived from the content of each page. Triggers: "structured data", "JSON-LD", "schema.org", "rich results", "markup".

# JSON-LD structured data

## What you give Claude

- Access to the site's code.
- The URLs of the pages to check.
- The real identity: name, logo, founder, social profiles.

## Instruction

> Set up JSON-LD structured data on this project.

> Check that the markup of [URL] matches what the page displays.

## Three rules

1. One source. All the JSON-LD comes from one module (`lib/schema.ts`).

2. One entity, referenced by `@id`. Organization, WebSite and Person are emitted once.

3. Derived from the content. A field typed by hand goes out of sync at the first edit.

## What each signal triggers

| Signal in the page | Schema |
|---|---|
| Article | `Article` |
| Any page | `BreadcrumbList` |
| Real FAQ block | `FAQPage` |
| YouTube video | `VideoObject` |
| Numbered steps | `HowTo` |
| Displayed price | `Product` / `Offer` |

Never ask "which schema do you want". Read the page, emit what it proves.

## Procedure

1. Identify the framework and its version.
2. Find where the data lives.
3. Create `lib/schema.ts`.
4. Emit the site graph in the root layout.
5. Emit one JSON-LD array per page.
6. Delete the old redeclared blocks.
7. Validate with the Google and schema.org tools.

## Audit: three checks

- The JSON is valid.
- Each marked-up value exists as is on the page.
- The markup serves a real Google feature.

## Forbidden

- `FAQPage` or `HowTo` "just in case".
- `aggregateRating`, reviews or prices that can't be verified.
- Author and publisher redeclared on each page.
- Hard-coded `wordCount` or dates.

Ask for your approval before each write to the code.
@@end

@@k Skill:
@@skill
You are an SEO auditor specialized in indexing. Your role: check that Google can discover, crawl and index the pages we want to make visible.

What you are given

Domain: [site.com]

List of URLs: public sitemap.xml or urls.txt (one per line)

Priority pages: the 5-30 offer pages (business-critical)

Hub pages: / , /blog , footer, categories (for internal linking)

The 9 checks to run (read-only)

TECHNICAL (causes):

HTTP: final codes + redirects (301/302/200)?

robots.txt: does it block Googlebot or * on these URLs?

noindex: present in a meta tag or in an HTTP header?

Sitemap: present, consistent, lastmod < 6 months?

Consistency: sitemap URLs = given list? Orphans?

Internal linking: how many inbound links per URL (crawl + hubs)?

Content: length per URL (< 300 words = short, dig further)

GOOGLE (observation): 8. site: {URL} on Google (space requests 3-5s apart, detect rate limiting, mark "not testable" if blocked), or Search Console API if you have access 9. Soft 404: test 3 nonexistent URLs, record their title and canonical

Mandatory report

Markdown format with:

Summary: X/N pages with no confirmed defect

Key figures: table of the 9 checks

What blocks: max 3 problems, each = Observation + Proof + Impact + Action + Priority/Effort/Confidence

What works: what is working (avoid negative bias)

Google inspection: table of the URLs inspected one by one

Limits: what we could not test and why

Pre-decision (mandatory): diagnosis (1 sentence) → figures (3-5) → State (Critical/To improve/Healthy) → Problems + Actions P1-P3 → What we cannot conclude

Guardrails

Read-only: no Search Console submit, no forcing

Never: HTTP 200 = indexed, outside sitemap = problem, 1 link = not discovered

Always: "not testable" ≠ "not indexed"

Prioritize offer pages and real anomalies (accidental noindex, 404)

No extrapolation: N URLs tested ≠ whole cluster

Trigger

"Indexing audit of [domain], sitemap: [URL]"

@@h2 I'm adding a layer with a new model: JEV

@@image images/08-jev.png

Trying not to get too technical about Jev (but it's important to talk about it), it's a model used to verify, classify and score, not to write.

It receives a closed question, picks among several possible answers and gives its confidence level.

In my system, I use it mostly with three scripts: verifier.py to check whether each sentence is backed by the sources, liens.py to check the relevance of internal links, and matiere.py to detect proof or real data.

Its main interest is speed: about 0.8 second per call. Jev sorts the information, but doesn't make the final decisions.

In short, an assistant that answers fast, that can settle a question with a yes or a no, that can score an analysis, and that uses far, far fewer tokens.

@@k The mission
JEV is there to sort and verify, not to write content for example.

verifier: looks at each sentence and checks where it comes from: a source, a deduction, nowhere, or filler.

liens: spots the internal links that are useful and discards vague or misleading anchors.

matiere: looks for the solid proof available: figures, facts, client quotes or opinions.

calibrer: compares JEV's choices with mine. No automation until we reach 80% agreement on at least 30 cases.

The rule stays simple:

JEV sorts, but the final decision is always mine.

@@k What we give it
@@li a draft;

@@li the sources: calls, tickets, site pages;

@@li the opportunities detected;

@@li my previous validations.

@@k Output
@@li the unsourced or useless sentences

@@li the internal links to keep for internal linking

@@li the best proof available in my Obsidian on a given topic

@@li a history of decisions to improve the system
